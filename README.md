# Hi, I’m Katia Gesuero

**I’m learning to code while building Vicinora, the app I wish I had to help me care for my mum.**

## How I got here

I spent years running beauty salons, managing teams and working with clients. When I ran my own salon, a single day could take me from checking the accounts to rearranging shifts, listening to a client or making a decision with my team. One habit from those days has stayed with me. When I build something, I ask the people who’ll use it how it works for them.

## What I’m building

In my family, a simple question like “Who’s taking Mum to her appointment?” can send us scrolling through a group chat for the answer. That’s where the idea for **Vicinora** came from. I want anyone caring for a family member to be able to open the app and see at a glance what needs doing today, who’s taking care of it and what’s already done.

## What I’m learning

I’m studying data analysis with **Excel, Python and SQL**. After years of looking at the numbers behind my salon, I want to understand how those numbers come together. I build dashboards in Excel, practise working with data in Python and use SQL to learn how to ask better questions of a database.

This profile is my working notebook. It’s where I share exercises, experiments and projects as they take shape.
